Powering products at

One registry, every surface

The same five attributes describe a marketing page, a dashboard and a printed invoice — and the same audit keeps all three honest.

  • Themes that follow the brand

    Twenty-seven themes read one token layer. Swap the stylesheet and every component, backdrop and chart re-colours itself.

  • Audited before it ships

    Roles, names, contrast and motion fallbacks are checked against each component's manifest, on every page, in one command.

  • No runtime

    Markup and a stylesheet. Nothing to hydrate, nothing to bundle, nothing to wait for.

  • Right-to-left ready

    Logical properties throughout; a locale is a document attribute.

  • Composable

    Patterns are made of primitives you can read, not abstractions you must learn.

  • Private registries

    Publish your own components next to the maintained ones.

Billing and plans

How seats, invoices and upgrades work. Numbered so support can point you at the right answer.

Need an invoice? Write to billing

How are seats counted?
Anyone who can edit a project is a seat. Viewers and commenters are free, without limit.
Can I change plans mid-cycle?
Yes. Upgrades apply immediately and are prorated; downgrades take effect at the next renewal.
Do you offer annual billing?
Annual plans are billed up front at two months off the monthly price.
Which payment methods do you accept?
Cards and bank transfer. Purchase orders are available on annual plans.
How do I cancel?
From the workspace settings, any time. Your data stays exportable for ninety days after the last renewal.
