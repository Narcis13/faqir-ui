What's in the kiln

One brand in, a whole system out

Kiln reads your brand once and keeps every surface in step — the marketing site, the product and the slide deck someone makes on a Friday.

  • Themes from a single seed

    Pick an accent and a handful of character axes. Kiln writes a contrast-checked theme, light and dark, and refuses one that looks like a rival's.

  • Audited before it ships

    Every page is checked for roles, names, contrast and motion fallbacks in one pass.

  • No runtime tax

    Markup and a stylesheet. Nothing to hydrate.

  • Every locale

    Right-to-left is one attribute.

  • Composable all the way down

    Patterns are components nested in components, never copies — fix a button once and every page has the fix.

  • Agents welcome

    Every component ships a manifest an agent can read, so generated pages come out on-brand the first time.
