Choreography tokens

One file of timing, read by every component that moves.

No component spells an easing or a duration of its own. A brisk theme shortens --motion-reveal-distance and --motion-reveal-duration; a theatrical one lengthens both and raises --motion-stagger. Setting --motion-ambient-play: paused on :root, or on any subtree, freezes every loop there without touching a transition.

Entrances

Reveal: seven effects, three triggers, one stagger.

An entrance plays once on load by default. data-trigger="scroll" scrubs it with the scrollbar on a view timeline. A data-state the page writes holds it back and releases it. Reduced motion and print resolve every one to “already arrived”, and markup without a script is never hidden.

Emphasis

Highlight: the words a headline wants read first.

Every effect is a background under or clipped to real text, so it stays selectable, searchable and announced once. Forced colours restore plain text.

Ambient

Light and movement nobody had to ask for.

Backdrops, glows and marquees run on --motion-ambient-duration and stop on --motion-ambient-play. They are still under reduced motion and gone in forced colours and print.

Additive properties

New behaviour on the components you already have.

Card hovers and a media slot, button and link effects, image hovers and live badges. Every one is a new attribute; markup without it renders exactly as it did.

Engine transitions

Eight l-transition presets.

Five are new: slide-down, slide-start, slide-end, blur and flip. Enter is slower and eased out, leave is faster and eased in, both from motion tokens. The engine only stamps the phases; the CSS animates.

The reactive engine
Restraint

Everything here knows when to stop.

  • Reduced motion. Every entrance resolves to its final state, every loop holds still, and a marquee becomes ordinary wrapped content.
  • Forced colours. Gradients, glows and backdrops step aside; highlighted text becomes plain text in the system colours.
  • Print. Nothing animates and nothing is hidden waiting for a scroll that will never come.
  • Graceful support. Scroll-linked behaviour sits behind @supports. Where view timelines are missing, content is simply there.
  • Pausable. A marquee pauses under the pointer and the keyboard, and every ambient loop answers to one token.
